# Expense policy / 报销规则

This is synthetic test material, not a production policy.

报销审批需要发票和业务用途说明。单次市内交通报销上限为 300 元。
Expense approval requires an invoice and a business-purpose statement. The limit for a single local-transport claim is CNY 300.

请在每周五 16:00 前提交报销申请。材料齐全后，财务组通常在 5 个工作日内完成审核。
Submit expense requests before Friday at 16:00. Finance normally reviews complete submissions within 5 business days.

海外差旅需要先获得直属经理的书面批准。外币金额按消费当日的公司记账汇率换算。
International travel requires prior written approval from the direct manager. Foreign-currency amounts use the company accounting exchange rate on the transaction date.

遗失发票的申请需补充付款凭证，并由直属经理和财务组共同确认。
Missing-invoice claims require proof of payment and approval from both the direct manager and Finance.
