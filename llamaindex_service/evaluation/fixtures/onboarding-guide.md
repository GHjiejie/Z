# Onboarding guide / 入职指南

This is synthetic test material, not a production policy.

新员工应在首个工作日启用多因素认证 MFA，并领取公司电脑。设备交接由 IT 服务台办理。
New employees enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) and collect a company laptop on their first workday. The IT service desk handles device handover.

代码仓库访问权限需要直属经理审批，由平台工程组开通。入职培训在每周一 14:00 举行。
Repository access requires approval from the direct manager and is provisioned by Platform Engineering. Orientation takes place every Monday at 14:00.

VPN 访问仅允许使用受管理的公司设备。遇到 MFA 重置问题，请联系 IT 服务台核验身份。
VPN access is permitted only from managed company devices. Contact the IT service desk for identity verification when an MFA reset is required.

试用期目标需在入职后的第 10 个工作日前，与直属经理共同确认。
Agree probation objectives with the direct manager before the tenth business day after joining.
